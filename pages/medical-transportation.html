Dialysis, Therapy & Recurring Appointments

Medical transportation works best as a routine, not a scramble. If you travel to dialysis three times a week, physical therapy twice a week, or a standing series of appointments, set the schedule once with Leeway Rides and we will be there for every trip — same dependable service, confirmed in advance. Recurring medical schedules are one of our specialties.

Appointments We Commonly Serve

Doctor and specialist visits, dialysis treatments, physical and occupational therapy, outpatient procedures, hospital discharge pickups, and pharmacy or follow-up trips — across Shelby, Kings Mountain, Gastonia, Forest City, and Charlotte. Our medical transportation is ambulatory: riders should be able to get in and out of a standard vehicle, with a companion welcome to ride along. If you are unsure whether a trip fits, call us at 980-470-7973 and we will talk it through honestly before you book.

For Families & Caregivers

Booking for a parent or loved one? We will confirm the pickup details with you, and your rider rides with a professional local driver who treats every passenger with patience and respect. Facilities and discharge planners: see our Partners page for recurring transportation arrangements.
